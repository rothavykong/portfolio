About

The messy middle is where I work.

I grew up in Phnom Penh and got my first taste of "scale" at 17, when a podcast I helped co-coordinate crossed 176,000 listeners. I learned that paying attention to an audience is the same skill as reading data — and that the people who can do both and tell the story usually win.

Three years later I'm a Lincoln Scholar at Centre College studying Data Science, doing marketing analytics internships at two organizations on two continents, and founding a campus computing club because the one I wanted didn't exist yet.

I'm drawn to the messy middle: the place where a logistic regression has to become a business decision, where a 100K-row GA4 export has to become a marketing strategy, where a TikTok hook has to become a hypothesis. Most data work fails not at the modeling step but at the translation step — so that's where I spend my time.

Outside of class I run front-of-house ops for a 1,500-seat performing arts center, lead a Girls Who Code chapter, and build things on the side.
